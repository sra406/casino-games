    How to play

    • Two private cards each, five shared cards on the board.
    • Make the best five-card hand from any of your seven.
    • Betting rounds: pre-flop, flop, turn, and river.
    • No limit: raise at least the last raise, up to all your credits.
    • All-in players can only win the pots they covered.
    • Out of time: you check if you can, otherwise you fold.
    • Leaving during a hand folds it.
