CASINO RULES

Bets & payouts

  • Come-out: pass wins on 7 or 11, loses on 2, 3, 12; any other total sets the point.
  • Point: pass wins if the point repeats before a 7. Don't pass is the reverse; 12 on the come-out pushes.
  • Come / don't come work the same way once a point is on.
  • Odds pay true odds: 2:1 on 4/10, 3:2 on 5/9, 6:5 on 6/8 (3-4-5x; lay up to 6x).
  • Place: 9:5 on 4/10, 7:5 on 5/9, 7:6 on 6/8. Big 6/8 pay 1:1. Payouts round down, so bet place 6/8 in multiples of 6 and 4/5/9/10 in multiples of 5 for full pay.
  • Field: 3, 4, 9, 10, 11 pay 1:1; 2 pays 2:1; 12 pays 3:1.
  • Hard 4/10 pay 7:1, hard 6/8 pay 9:1. Any 7 4:1, any craps 7:1, 3 or 11 15:1, 2 or 12 30:1.
  • Place bets, hardways and come odds are off on the come-out roll.
  • Pass and come bets with a point can't be taken down; leaving the table loses them.
